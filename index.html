About Us

Your Global Logistics Partner Based in Shenzhen

Shenzhen Fulaiying International Freight Agency Co., Ltd. (深圳市福涞迎国际货运代理有限公司) is a premier freight forwarding and logistics company headquartered in the heart of Shenzhen's international trade district. With over a decade of industry experience, we provide end-to-end supply chain solutions that connect businesses across China to markets worldwide.

Our strategic location in Shenzhen — one of the world's busiest port cities and a global manufacturing hub — gives us unique advantages in speed, cost, and network connectivity. From ocean freight consolidation to time-critical air cargo, rail freight to South America to cross-border trucking into Southeast Asia, our team of logistics professionals ensures your cargo moves efficiently, safely, and on schedule.

What We Do

Comprehensive Logistics Services

End-to-end freight solutions tailored to your cargo, timeline, and budget.

Ocean Freight

YML & SMA consolidation from all major Chinese ports. Competitive rates, reliable schedules, and real-time tracking for your sea cargo.

Air Freight

Time-critical air cargo solutions from Shenzhen, China. Express, standard, and economy options to global destinations.

Rail Freight

Fast, cost-effective rail freight connecting Shenzhen to Paraguay and South America.

Cross-border Trucking

Dedicated cross-border trucking to Vietnam, Laos, Myanmar, and Central Asia. Fast transit times with full customs documentation support.

Customs Clearance

Expert customs brokerage for import and export. Full documentation, classification, valuation, and compliance services for smooth border crossings.

Warehousing & Distribution

Secure warehousing in Shenzhen with inventory management, pick & pack, labeling, and last-mile distribution across China and beyond.
